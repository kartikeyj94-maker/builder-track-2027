# B2B AI SaaS — Three-Touch Outbound Sequence

## Target audience
Heads of Customer Support or CX at US B2B SaaS companies
with 50–500 employees.

## Touch 1 — Day 1
Goal: Introduce a relevant operational problem.

## Touch 2 — Day 4
Goal: Share a practical use case.

## Touch 3 — Day 8
Goal: Offer a simple next step.

## Sequence Stop Rules
1. Stop outreach immediately if the prospect opts out.
2. Stop automated follow-ups when the prospect replies.
3. Stop the sequence when a meeting is booked.
4. Do not enrol leads without qualification and human approval.
5. Prevent duplicate enrolment of the same contact.
6. Respect applicable email compliance requirements.
7. Stop after Touch 3 if there is no response.
